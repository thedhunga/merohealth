# Compliance gap register

No row below is a legal conclusion. Nepal-qualified counsel, licensed clinicians, pharmacists, privacy specialists, and relevant authorities must resolve these items before launch.

| Area | Unresolved decision | Interim engineering control | Launch gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telemedicine | permitted modalities, disclosures, record duties | configurable consent and jurisdiction flags | counsel + clinical sign-off |
| Licensing | verification source, renewal, cross-jurisdiction practice | expiring credential assertions; deny when unverified | authoritative process tested |
| E-prescribing | signature validity, medicine classes, retention | signed state machine; controlled items disabled | counsel/pharmacy approval |
| Pharmacy/delivery | substitution, restricted medicine and courier rules | pharmacist review and policy rules | operating procedure approved |
| Minor/guardian | age thresholds and authority evidence | relationship model; high-risk actions disabled | counsel approval |
| Retention/deletion | mandatory clinical, financial, audit periods | policy-based holds and deletion workflow | retention schedule approved |
| Data location | hosting and cross-border transfer limits | deployment region abstraction; vendor inventory | DPIA/legal approval |
| Emergency claims | validated numbers/facilities and uptime expectation | no unverified numbers; template disclaimer | clinical/legal validation |
| Lab results | release, abnormal flags, clinician review | non-diagnostic flags; configurable hold | laboratory governance |
| Payments/refunds | taxes, settlement, consumer protection | configurable ledger; mock provider | finance/legal approval |
| Advertising | healthcare promotion restrictions | no condition-targeted ads | policy approved |
| AI governance | required disclosure, oversight, incident reporting | versioned prompts/models/evaluations | governance board approval |
| Accessibility | applicable legal/market obligations | WCAG 2.2 AA target; native audits | independent test |
| Training media | medical/legal review and accessibility | versioned review status, captions/transcript | content approval |
